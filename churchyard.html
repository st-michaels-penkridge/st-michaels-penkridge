Find a grave

Search everyone recorded in St Michael's churchyard by name, see where they lie on the churchyard plan, and view photographs of their memorial.

Records come from memorial inscriptions and the churchyard survey, so some names and dates may be missing. If you spot a mistake or can't find someone, please tell the parish office on 01785 714344.

Help look after the churchyard

The grave finder is free to use. If it has helped you, a gift helps the parish care for the churchyard and keep its records up to date.
