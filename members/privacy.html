Privacy notice

How the PCC looks after the details of the people on our rotas.

Draft for the PCC. This notice needs the PCC's agreement, and the details in square brackets filling in, before any real names go into the members area.

Who looks after your details

The Parochial Church Council (PCC) of St Michael and All Angels, Penkridge, registered charity 1131086, is responsible for your details. In data protection law it is the data controller. You can reach us through the parish office: The Rectory, New Road, Penkridge ST19 5DN, 01785 714344.

What we keep

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, only if you add one
  • Which teams you are on, and whether you lead one
  • Your rota dates, swap requests and the dates you are away
  • A record of changes made in the members area: who changed what, and when

We do not keep addresses, dates of birth, health details, anything about children, or safeguarding information.

Why we keep it

To organise the rotas that keep services running: who is on when, and how to reach you about it. We do this because it is in the legitimate interests of the church and of the people who have offered to help. We do not use your details for anything else, and we never sell them or share them for marketing.

Who can see it

Other members see your name on the rotas. The leads of your rotas and the parish office can also see your email address, phone number and away dates. Nobody outside the members area can see any of it.

Where it is kept

On Supabase, a database service, in a data centre in [the UK or the EU, to confirm]. Sign-in links and rota reminders are sent by email through [email service, to confirm].

How long we keep it

Until you leave the rotas or ask us to remove you. Old rota dates are deleted after 13 months, away dates a month after they end, and requests to join after 60 days.

Your rights

You can see everything we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. When you are signed in, My details lets you download your data or delete your account straight away. You can also ask the parish office. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

The members area keeps a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies.
