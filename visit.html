On foot and by train

The church is in the middle of the village, on St Michael's Square. Penkridge railway station is a short walk away.

Parking and access

For parking and step-free access, please call the parish office before you come and we'll help you plan your visit.

Parish office

The Rectory, New Road, Penkridge ST19 5DN. Call 01785 714344 or message us on the parish Facebook page.

Visiting a grave?

Search the churchyard by name before you come, and see where the grave is on the churchyard plan.
