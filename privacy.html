Privacy and cookies

This website doesn't ask for your name or email, and it doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we are

This website is run by the Parochial Church Council (PCC) of St Michael and All Angels, Penkridge, registered charity 1131086. The PCC is responsible for any personal information the parish holds.

What this site collects

Reading the website doesn't need any personal information. Like every website, the server that hosts it briefly records technical details such as your IP address to keep the site running and secure.

If you contact the parish by phone or email, we use your details only to reply to you.

Cookies

The public pages don't set cookies. Your browser may remember small settings, such as the last notice you saw, on your own device only.

If you sign in to the members area, your browser remembers that you are signed in, on your device only.

Other services on this site

  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering, so your browser connects to Google to download it.
  • Google Maps loads only if you press "Show the map".
  • Gravefindr runs the churchyard search. When you search, your search is sent to Gravefindr.
  • The Parish Giving Scheme handles online giving on its own website, under its own privacy policy.

The members area

The members area for rotas and notices has its own privacy notice, which explains what it stores and why.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete any information the parish holds about you. Contact the parish office on 01785 714344. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
