Events calendar

Key dates for 2026 at St Michael's — movable feasts follow the Easter cycle; confirm service times on the notice sheet before you come.

Months of the year

January

  • 6 January · Epiphany — worship marking the visit of the Magi
  • 18–25 January · Week of Prayer for Christian Unity — joint services locally where arranged

February

  • 2 February · Candlemas — presentation of Christ in the Temple
  • 18 February · Ash Wednesday — start of Lent
  • from 18 February · Lent groups — study and prayer through the season

March

  • 15 March · Mothering Sunday — mid-Lent refreshment and flowers
  • 29 March–3 April · Holy Week — Palm Sunday, Maundy Thursday, Good Friday

April

  • 5 April · Easter Day — principal celebration of the resurrection
  • 5 April–24 May · Easter season — festal worship through Pentecost

May

  • 10 May, 14 May · Rogation & Ascension — prayer for farms, homes, and community
  • May–June · Parish walks / outdoor worship — when weather allows

June

  • 4 June · Corpus Christi / thanksgiving — as marked in parish rota
  • June (date TBC) · Summer fête or open church — fundraising and welcome

July

  • July–August (dates TBC) · Holiday club or family activities — when volunteers available
  • July–August · Quiet summer services — check notice sheet

August

  • August (dates in rota) · Benefice services — shared worship across linked churches
  • August (dates TBC) · Churchyard tidy days — community volunteering

September

  • 29 September · Michaelmas — patronal festival for St Michael and All Angels
  • late September · Harvest preparation — gifts for local food banks

October

  • 11 October (typical) · Harvest festival — thanksgiving service with produce and hymns
  • 2 November · All Souls — remembrance of the faithful departed

November

  • 1–2 November · All Saints & All Souls — commemorations
  • 8 November · Remembrance Sunday — act of remembrance at the war memorial
  • 29 November · Advent Sunday — start of the new church year

December

  • December (dates in notices) · Advent carols & crib services — families and schools welcome
  • 24–25 December · Christmas Eve & Christmas Day — crib, midnight/evening, and morning worship
  • December (date TBC) · Christingle — if held with uniformed groups or schools

Every week

  • Sunday — Parish Eucharist, 10:30 (see home page)
  • Weekdays — morning prayer or holy communion as advertised
  • Special services — weddings, baptisms, and funerals by arrangement
