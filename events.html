What's on

Sunday worship every week, Messy Church and coffee mornings through the month, and the big days of the church year.

Every week

Every week

Sunday, 10:30

Holy Communion on the first, second, fourth and fifth Sundays of the month, usually finishing between 11:30 and 12.

Third Sunday

By arrangement

Baptisms, weddings and funerals. Speak to the Rector on 01785 714344.

Through the month

Messy Church and coffee mornings

Messy Church

Church for families, with crafts and activities, a short celebration together and food to share. It's free, and children come with their grown-ups.

Next dates are on the parish Facebook page.

Coffee mornings

Tea, coffee, cake and a chat in the church. Everyone is welcome, whether or not you come on Sundays.

Next dates are on the parish Facebook page.

The church year

Through the year

The church year follows the seasons. Easter moves each year, so the dates in spring change with it.

Winter

  • 6 January, Epiphany
  • 2 February, Candlemas

Lent

  • Ash Wednesday, the start of Lent
  • Mothering Sunday, the fourth Sunday of Lent

Easter

  • Holy Week, from Palm Sunday to Good Friday
  • Easter Day
  • Ascension and Pentecost, 40 and 50 days after Easter

Autumn

  • 29 September, Michaelmas, our patronal festival
  • October, Harvest festival

November

  • 1 and 2 November, All Saints and All Souls
  • Remembrance Sunday

Advent and Christmas

  • Advent Sunday, the start of the church year
  • 24 and 25 December, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day
