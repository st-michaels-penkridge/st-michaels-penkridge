Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website, whatever device or access needs they have.

What we've done

  • Pages are built to work on phones, tablets and computers, and when zoomed in.
  • Text is in sentence case with strong contrast, and headings follow a clear order.
  • Images have descriptions for screen readers, and every link and button can be reached with a keyboard.
  • Animation is reduced if your device asks for less motion.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

Known problems

  • The Google map, when shown, is hard to use with a screen reader. The address and a directions link are always shown beside it.
  • Some older photographs and engravings have only short descriptions.

Visiting the church

For access to the building itself, see Visit us, or call the parish office.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website doesn't work for you, call the parish office on 01785 714344 and we'll put it right.
