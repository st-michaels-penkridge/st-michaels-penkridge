Baptisms, weddings and funerals

St Michael's has marked the big moments of Penkridge life for centuries. Whatever you're planning, the Rector will talk it through with you.

Baptism

Baptism, sometimes called christening, welcomes a child or an adult into the Christian family. To arrange one, for your child or for yourself, talk to the Rector.

Weddings

You can marry at St Michael's if you live in the parish or come to church here. You can also marry here if you have a family connection to the church, for example if you were baptised here or a parent or grandparent was married here.

The Rector can tell you whether you qualify and what happens next.

Funerals

A funeral can be held at St Michael's whether or not the person came to church. Your funeral director can contact us for you, or you can call the Rector yourself.

Speak to the Rector, Gregory Yerbury, on 01785 714344.
