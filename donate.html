Support St Michael's

St Michael's has been at the heart of Penkridge for centuries. It is kept open, warm and cared for by the gifts of people who love it, and every gift, large or small, helps.

Ways to give

Give regularly

A monthly gift by Direct Debit through the Church of England's Parish Giving Scheme helps us plan ahead. You can choose to raise it each year with inflation, and Gift Aid is claimed for you.

Set up regular giving

Give once

Give during a service or whenever you visit the church. If you would like to make a one-off gift another way, or give in memory of someone, please contact the parish office and we will help.

Contact the parish office

Add Gift Aid

If you pay UK income tax, Gift Aid lets the church claim an extra 25p for every £1 you give, at no cost to you. Tick the Gift Aid box when you set up your gift, or ask the parish office for a declaration form.

How Gift Aid works

What your gift does

  • Keeps the church open, heated, lit and insured for worship and for visitors
  • Cares for a historic building, from the roof and windows to the organ
  • Supports services, music, baptisms, weddings and funerals
  • Looks after the churchyard and its records, so families can find the graves of people they love
  • Funds our work with children, families and the wider Penkridge community

Leaving a gift in your will

A gift in your will helps St Michael's stay at the heart of Penkridge for generations to come. If you are thinking about it, please speak to the parish office in confidence.
