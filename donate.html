Give to St Michael's

St Michael's has been at the heart of Penkridge for about 800 years. It is paid for mainly by the people who give to it.

What your gift does

  • Keeps the church open, heated, lit and insured for worship and for visitors
  • Cares for a Grade I building, from the roof and windows to the organ
  • Supports Sunday services, Messy Church, music, baptisms, weddings and funerals
  • Looks after the churchyard and its records, so families can find the people they love

Give monthly

A regular gift by Direct Debit is the most useful way to give, because it lets the parish plan ahead. Gift Aid is claimed for you.

Set up a monthly gift

Through the Parish Giving Scheme. You can change or stop your gift at any time.

Other ways to give

  • Give once

    Give during a service or when you visit the church. To give another way, call the parish office on 01785 714344.

  • Add Gift Aid

    If you pay UK income tax, Gift Aid adds 25p to every £1 you give, at no cost to you. Tick the Gift Aid box when you give, or ask the parish office for a form. How Gift Aid works

  • In memory

    Give in memory of someone buried in the churchyard or remembered at St Michael's. The parish office can help.

  • In your will

    A gift in your will helps St Michael's for generations to come. Please speak to the parish office in confidence.
